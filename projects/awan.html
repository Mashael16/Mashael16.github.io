Overview

AWAN is a web platform for businesses in Saudi Arabia that manage compliance documents across multiple branches. It brings registrations, licenses, employee certificates, renewals, and branch readiness into one place.

164automated tests

The problem

A company with many branches must track documents with different requirements and expiry dates. A missed municipal license or health certificate can leave a branch unprepared for inspection. Spreadsheets alone do not clearly explain which document creates the risk or who is responsible for resolving it.

How I solved it

AWAN stores documents centrally, evaluates branch readiness automatically, and reports one of three states: Ready, At Risk, or Not Ready, with the reason. It turns renewal needs into assigned tasks and preserves previous document versions so each renewal has a history.

What I built

Readiness rules

Evaluates expiry dates and document importance to determine each branch’s status and explain why.

Clear renewal ownership

Assigns renewal tasks to owners or branch managers and tracks their progress.

Document history

Keeps earlier versions when a license or certificate is renewed.

Role-based security

Owners see all branches; branch managers only access their assigned locations. Files are checked before access.

Flexible operations

Supports custom document types, reminder windows, Excel reports, and a responsive Arabic RTL interface.

A key engineering decision

I defined readiness and renewal rules before implementing them. For example, simply creating a renewal task is not evidence of progress. I also used database constraints to protect the main-branch rules even if an application form is bypassed.

Challenges & lessons

Turning policy into consistent logic

I wrote detailed rules and edge cases for Ready, At Risk, and Not Ready.

Protecting business invariants

Database-level constraints enforce main-branch behavior rather than depending only on form validation.

Testing important workflows

I built 164 automated tests for permissions, readiness calculations, and document-renewal flows.

Results

Capstone project at Tuwaiq Academy, graded with full marks, and live online.
